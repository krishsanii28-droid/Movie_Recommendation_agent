# Mood reference

## Mood → tones

Use the tones for the user's goal. "Avoid" tones clash with that goal.

| Feeling | Stay (lean in) | Shift (change it) | Avoid when shifting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tired / drained | gentle, calming, cosy, comforting, warm | cosy, light, funny, feel-good, warm | intense, hard-hitting, dark, slow-burn |
| Stressed | cathartic, intense, tense | calming, funny, light, comforting, feel-good | tense, scary, intense, dark |
| Anxious | cathartic, poignant, gentle | comforting, gentle, funny, warm, calming | tense, scary, eerie, dark |
| Lonely | melancholic, bittersweet, gentle, poignant | warm, heartwarming, feel-good, comforting, funny | dark, melancholic |
| Heartbroken | bittersweet, melancholic, romantic, cathartic | funny, uplifting, feel-good, energetic, hopeful | romantic, melancholic, bittersweet |
| Sad / down | melancholic, bittersweet, poignant, cathartic | uplifting, heartwarming, funny, hopeful, feel-good | dark, hard-hitting, gritty |
| Angry / frustrated | intense, energetic, fast-paced, cathartic | funny, light, calming, feel-good | intense, gritty, dark |
| Scared / uneasy | scary, eerie, tense | comforting, funny, light, warm | scary, eerie, tense |
| Bored | thrilling, twisty, energetic, quirky, adventurous | (same) | slow-burn |
| Curious / "make me think" | mind-bending, thought-provoking, twisty | — | — |
| Nostalgic | nostalgic, bittersweet, warm, romantic | feel-good, energetic | — |
| Romantic / date night | romantic, warm, feel-good | funny, adventurous | — |
| Party / excited | party, funny, energetic, musical | calming, gentle | slow-burn, melancholic, dark |
| Happy | feel-good, funny, energetic, uplifting | thought-provoking, poignant | dark, hard-hitting |
| Calm | gentle, calming, warm, slow-burn | thrilling, energetic | — |

Energy: **low** → favour films under ~2h10, gentle or light. **High** → energetic, fast-paced.
Company: **family** → family-friendly, no gore or sexual content; **friends** → party, funny;
**partner** → romantic or warm (unless heartbroken).
